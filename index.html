// 04 CRAFTSMANSHIP

THE PHYSICS OF
LUXURY DRAPING

Standard event decor treats fabric as a flat wall cover. At Élan, we treat fabric as an architectural medium governed by tension, gravity, shadow depth, and micro-fold pleating.

01

Weighted Gravity System

Internal hemline weights ensure straight vertical tension line and consistent floor pooling.

02

Lighting Response Optimization

Fabrics selected specifically for light absorption versus specular reflection ratios.

03

Modular Rigging Architecture

Freestanding telescoping frames with zero impact on venue structural walls.
